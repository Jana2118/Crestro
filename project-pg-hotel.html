PG / Hotel Platform

About the project

Unified discovery and booking for paying-guest accommodations and hotels — search, compare, and book with verified listings.

The challenge

Students and professionals juggle brokers and scattered listings. Owners lack a clean digital channel.

Key features

Search across PGs and hotels with location, budget, and amenity filters
Verified listings with photos, pricing, and reviews
In-app booking and payments
Owner dashboards for occupancy and listings
Map search and nearby recommendations
Messaging between seekers and hosts

Impact

Search became simpler, owners reached more guests digitally, and direct booking reduced broker dependency.

Who it serves

A two-sided marketplace: clarity for seekers, occupancy tools for owners.

The longer story

Every product on this page started as a constraint: too much manual work, too much risk, or too little clarity for users. We treated those constraints as design inputs — not afterthoughts — and shaped architecture, interface, and delivery around them.

The result is not only a feature list. It is a system that operators can trust, users can understand, and teams can extend without starting over.
