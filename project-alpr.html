License Plate Recognition

About the project

Real-time automatic license plate recognition from camera feeds for access control, parking, tolling, and security.

The challenge

Manual vehicle logging does not scale across gates, lots, or multi-site campuses.

Key features

Real-time detection and recognition from live video
Robust across lighting, angles, and plate styles
Timestamped entry/exit logs
Integrations with access control, parking, and toll systems
Alerts for watchlists and blacklisted plates
Multi-camera and multi-site support

Impact

Identification became automated, manual checks dropped, and tracking speed and accuracy improved.

Who it serves

Parking operators, campuses, logistics yards, and security teams.

The longer story

Every product on this page started as a constraint: too much manual work, too much risk, or too little clarity for users. We treated those constraints as design inputs — not afterthoughts — and shaped architecture, interface, and delivery around them.

The result is not only a feature list. It is a system that operators can trust, users can understand, and teams can extend without starting over.
