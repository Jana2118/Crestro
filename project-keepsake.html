Newborn Keepsake Application

About the project

Help parents capture early milestones — photos, growth, first words, and moments — in one keepsake they can save, share, or print.

The challenge

Memories scatter across camera rolls and chat threads. Without structure, the earliest days are the easiest to lose.

Key features

Milestone timeline for growth and firsts
Photo and video journaling with timestamps
Printable albums, cards, and book templates
Family sharing and collaborative entries
Gentle reminders for milestones and checkups
Private-by-default design

Impact

Parents kept early memories organized, shared keepsakes with family, and spent less time hunting scattered media.

Who it serves

Warm, simple product design focused on moments — not feeds or noise.

The longer story

Every product on this page started as a constraint: too much manual work, too much risk, or too little clarity for users. We treated those constraints as design inputs — not afterthoughts — and shaped architecture, interface, and delivery around them.

The result is not only a feature list. It is a system that operators can trust, users can understand, and teams can extend without starting over.
